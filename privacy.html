Privacy Policy

Life Tech SG respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Last updated: 31 July 2026.

1. Who we are

Life Tech SG ("we", "us", "our") is a smart home consultancy and retailer based at 33 Mohamed Sultan Road, #02-01, Singapore 238977. This policy explains what personal data we collect through our website and services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what we need to help you:

  • Details you give us when you submit our guide-download form, book a demo, or contact us: your name, email address, WhatsApp or mobile number, and your type of housing.
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, email, or our contact links.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends automatically when you visit the site (such as device and browser type), used only to keep the site working well.

We do not collect payment card details through this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To send you the smart home guide you requested and respond to your enquiry.
  • To prepare recommendations and quotations for your home.
  • To arrange demos, site visits, and installation.
  • With your consent, to send occasional tips, product updates, or offers. You can opt out at any time.
  • To maintain and improve our website and services.

4. Consent and PDPA

By submitting your details to us, you consent to our collection, use, and disclosure of your personal data for the purposes above. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (see section 11). Withdrawing consent may mean we are unable to provide certain services, such as sending you the guide or a quotation.

5. Disclosure of your information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only where necessary with:

  • Trusted service providers who help us operate, such as our email delivery provider (to send you the guide) and website hosting provider.
  • Installers and partners involved in fulfilling your order, such as your appointed electrician or the LINE8 installation team, where relevant to your project.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your browser's request in the ordinary course of serving those fonts. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA, you may request access to the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to stop using it. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

10. Third-party links

Our site links to third-party services such as our social media pages and Google. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

11. Contact us

For any privacy question or request, contact us at sales@lifetechsg.com or WhatsApp 8854 7512.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
